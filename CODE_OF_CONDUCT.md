# Code of conduct

cubr is for every cuber, whatever their speed, age or background. In this repository's issues, pull requests and
discussions:

- **Be kind, and assume good faith.** People come from different places and speak different languages. A
  beginner's question is as welcome as anyone else's.
- **Disagree with ideas, not people.** Give your reasons, and accept that a decision can go the other way.
- **No harassment:** no insults, slurs, sexual content, threats, or sharing anyone's personal details; no piling on,
  and no unwanted attention once someone has asked you to stop.
- **Stay on topic,** without spam or advertising.

On cubr.dev itself, race chat included, the [terms](https://cubr.dev/terms) apply.

## Reporting

Email **hello@cubr.dev** with "conduct" in the subject. Say what happened and where, with links. Reports are read by
the maintainer only and kept confidential; you'll get a reply within a week.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, lock a thread, and block an account from this repository, for a
while or for good, depending on what happened. A first, minor slip usually gets a word, not a ban.
